First Soccer Tournament at Auburn ⚽

Soccer Auburn Life

There's something special about stepping onto a soccer field after spending all week staring at loss curves and debugging CUDA errors. Last weekend, our lab team — a ragtag mix of PhD students, postdocs, and one brave undergrad — entered Auburn's intramural soccer tournament.

The Setup

We called ourselves "Gradient Descent FC" (yes, really). Our jerseys were mismatched, our warm-up was chaotic, and our strategy meeting lasted exactly 90 seconds: "just pass and don't get hurt."

The Games

We played three matches. Won the first one 2-1 on a beautiful counter-attack (I may have screamed). Lost the second 0-3 to a team that clearly trained outside of research hours. The third was a 1-1 draw that felt like a victory.

The Takeaway

We didn't take home a trophy, but we took home something better — a reminder that stepping away from the screen and into the sun, laughing with colleagues over missed shots and lucky saves, is exactly the kind of reset a PhD brain needs.

Next tournament: we're coming for the title. 🏆
